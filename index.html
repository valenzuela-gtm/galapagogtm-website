Do you advise, or do you build?

Both. We plan it together first, then I build and deploy it inside your business: the plays, the pipeline, the CRM and the AI workflows that run them. The plan only matters once it's running.

Do you work in Spanish, and across the U.S. and LATAM?

Yes, in both languages. I've sold in the U.S. (a $38M enterprise book at Salesforce) and built revenue across LATAM, including for US and Israeli companies entering the region.

Is the diagnosis really FREE?

Yes. You get the written report and the call whether or not we work together. Not a fit? I'll tell you what to do instead.

How is it priced?

After the diagnosis, once we both know what you need. Everything after the diagnosis has a fixed investment and a fixed scope, agreed before we start. No hourly meter, no surprise invoices.

Why not hire a full-time sales leader?

Hire one once the motion works. Search, hiring and ramp take months, and a hire made before anyone knows what's broken adds cost to the problem. I fix the system first, so your hire has something to run.

Yes. We agree on the responsibilities and time your team needs.
