The GTM OS “five valleys of death”

Where good businesses lose their way.

From first interest to renewal, each step can stop growth.

GTM Partners' framework; questions and work adapted by Bernardo. These are places to investigate, not funding stages or failure probabilities. More than one can apply. The official framework ↗

Create → Market → Sell → Deliver → Renew → Expand

01 · Create → MarketA product buyers don't understand.

Buyers don't see why they need it or why now.

What I look at: the buyer, the problem, the offer and the message.

Why would the right customer buy?

02 · Market → SellInterest that never becomes revenue.

Meetings happen. Deals keep slipping.

What I look at: buyer readiness, sales conversations, pricing and follow up.

Why aren't buyers saying yes?

03 · Sell → DeliverThe sale promises more than the team delivers.

What customers get differs from what they bought.

What I look at: sales promises, handoff, onboarding and the first customer result.

Does the customer get what they bought?

04 · Deliver → RenewCustomers use it. Then they leave.

Customers use the product but can't justify renewing.

What I look at: expected results, actual results and why accounts leave.

Can customers see enough value to stay?

05 · Renew → ExpandCustomers stay. The accounts don't grow.

Every dollar of growth needs a new customer.

What I look at: other needs, new users and reasons to buy more.

Is there a good reason to buy more?
