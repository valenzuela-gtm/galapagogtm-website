OpenAI Ads pixel

The Site uses the OpenAI Ads pixel to measure whether our advertising leads to inquiries. It records page visits and a "lead" event when you send the diagnosis form or tap a WhatsApp, email or booking link on the Site. For visitors in Europe it loads only after you accept it in the cookie banner, and it never loads if your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal.

The GTM assessment linked from the Site runs on GTM Partners' website, under GTM Partners' own privacy policy. GTM Partners collects the information you enter there. If GTM Partners shares your results or contact details with us, we use them only to follow up on your request.
